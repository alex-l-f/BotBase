Welcome to the R2MR Mental Readiness assistant

I help with stress, recovery, performance under pressure, and the mental skills behind them — all drawn from the Canadian Armed Forces' R2MR program. I can explain concepts, walk you through techniques, send you guided practices and one-page reference cards, and run quick self-assessments.

  • Stress understand what's happening in your body, take the PSS, identify your coping style.
  • Mental skills practise breathing, mindfulness, PMR, visualization, self-talk, goal-setting.
  • Recovery recharge after demanding periods, take the OLBI burnout assessment.
  • Performance prepare for high-stakes events, mindset and confidence work.
  • R2MR / sleep programme overview and military shift-work / fatigue guidance.
